An open letter

What the people who love you already know

Dear Amaya,

One of the things I have always admired about you is that you probably have no idea how many people look up to you.

A while ago, I came across a study by psychologists Erica Boothby and Gus Cooney about something they call the liking gap. Their research found that people consistently underestimate how much others like, appreciate, and value them. We spend so much time noticing our own shortcomings that we rarely recognize the impression we leave on the people around us.

When I read that, I immediately thought of you.

I do not know if you have ever realized just how many people quietly look up to you. Maybe it is because the qualities that make you remarkable are the same ones you carry so naturally that you have stopped noticing them yourself.

I have watched you step into so many different roles—leader, student, teammate, mentor, and friend—and somehow you become a role model in every one of them. Not because you are trying to be one, but because your character does not change depending on who is watching.

Whether you are leading a room, supporting a friend, or simply existing in a space, you bring the same humility, kindness, and intention with you. That is not something you can teach. It is simply who you are.

One thing I have always admired about you is the way you carry responsibility. You have this almost impossible ability to balance everything that is asked of you while still making the people around you feel seen.

You are checking on everyone else, remembering the little things, showing up when it matters, leading by example, and somehow still finding room to chase your own dreams. To everyone else, you make the balancing act look effortless.

But I know it is not.

One of the greatest privileges our friendship has given me was not getting to know the version of you that everyone admires. Going far beyond "roses and thorns", it was getting to know the version that trusted me enough to put the strength down for a while—the version that did not feel like she had to have everything figured out.

Moments like that did not make me admire you any less. It made me admire you even more. It reminded me that strength is not pretending everything is okay. Sometimes strength is trusting someone enough to say that it is not.

Psychologists Amit Kumar and Nicholas Epley also found that people often underestimate how meaningful genuine expressions of appreciation are to others. That made me wonder how many people have walked away from a conversation with you feeling lighter than they did before.

How many people became more confident because you believed in them first. How many people found a place to belong simply because you welcomed them into your life.

My guess is more than you will ever know.

As you step into twenty-two, I do not hope that you become someone new. I hope you become even more convinced of the person you have already been.

I hope you continue leading with integrity, loving people wholeheartedly, and walking through life with the same quiet confidence that has inspired so many people around you. But I also hope this is the year you become gentler with yourself.

I hope you never feel like you have to earn your place by carrying everything alone.

The people who love you do not love you because you are the one who always has it together. They love your laugh, your curiosity, your faith, your compassion, your ability to make people feel at home, and your willingness to choose others even when no one is watching.

They love you.

Your accomplishments are extraordinary, but they were never the reason people look up to you. People look up to you because of the way you carry yourself while accomplishing them.

That is what people will remember.

So if the liking gap is real—and I think it is—let this be one small piece of evidence that your life has mattered in ways you may never be able to fully measure.

Happy 22nd Birthday, Amaya.
Keep blooming. 🌿

With love,
Mera [Dayna] Singh

Rooted in Haiti · Guided by spirit

Everything you carry

MY HAITIAN QUEEN! I wanted to include this part of you because your heritage is not just a fun fact about where you or your family is from— it is part of the history, pride, strength, and community you carry with you. I took some time to learn about a few Haitian symbols, and the more I read, the more I understood why they reminded me of you. So, here is my very unofficial and very loving interpretation.

🌺

Choublak

The hibiscus is bright, warm, and impossible to overlook. That reminded me of the way your presence brings life into a room without you ever having to force it.

🌴

The Royal Palm

The palm appears in Haiti’s coat of arms and is tied to freedom and endurance. To me, it represents being deeply rooted while still reaching upward—something I see in the way you carry yourself and continue growing.

🌱

The Garden

You nurture people the way a gardener nurtures life: patiently, intentionally, and with faith. This one is simply you. You pour into people, ideas, friendships, and communities, then somehow leave all of them better than you found them. Things genuinely grow around you.

🕯️

The Light

Your spirituality is the quiet flame within you: It is one of the things I admire most about you. It shows in your intuition, the way you protect your peace, and the way you help other people feel grounded without judging them.

🔵 🔴

Blue and Red

A visual echo of Haiti, representing identity, courage, heritage, and pride. These colors immediately bring Haiti to mind, but here they also represent the pride and confidence you have in who you are. You never have to shrink your identity to make anyone else comfortable.

🤝🏾

Unity

Your love gathers people together and reminds them that strength can be shared. It's no coincidence every org you took part in truly blossomed under your care.

“Inyon fè fòs” Unity makes strength
May every seed you plant return to you as peace, abundance, protection, purpose, and love.

November, 2024

The day we really became us

Never have I been more grateful for someone joining a team because, truthfully, I wasn't even sure I wanted to stay on Black Reign before you came. The moment you became liaison, everything just felt lighter. You somehow kept everyone together while still being just as unserious as the rest of us. I'll never forget us constantly finding an excuse to leave the practice room for "just a minute," only to end up laughing about absolutely nothing before walking back in like we hadn't disappeared. You had this way of making even the longest practices fun, and somehow every room you walked into felt more connected because of it. Looking back, it wasn't really step that made me stay; it was the friendships I found because of you. As much as I wish we had gotten close sooner, I'm grateful we met exactly when we did because I genuinely can't imagine my college experience without you in it.

♥
December, 2025

The sidequest we will never forget

The duality was peak here, first of many, might I add. I still don't know how we pulled this off. Party until late, barely sleep, somehow convince ourselves breakfast was still a great idea, squeeze in a power nap, and then magically make it to practice on time like nothing happened. It was one of those days that should've been a disaster but somehow became one of my favorite memories with you. I think that's our thing—we somehow make the most random sidequests become core memories. And I love that you'll always match my energy, no matter how ridiculous the plan is.
